About the Clinic

Sanjay Dental Clinic

At Sanjay Dental Clinic, we provide patient-focused dental care in a comfortable and professional environment. Our team is dedicated to helping you maintain good oral health and achieve a smile you can feel confident about.

We believe in clear communication, careful consultation, and treatment plans tailored to each patient’s needs. From routine check-ups to more specialized care, we focus on making your visit as pleasant and effective as possible.

Our Services

Dental Treatments

We offer a range of general dental care services to support your oral health. Please consult us to confirm the treatments best suited for your needs.

🔍

Dental Check-up

Comprehensive oral examination to assess your dental health and identify any concerns early.

✨

Teeth Cleaning

Professional scaling and polishing to remove plaque and keep your teeth and gums healthy.

🛠️

Tooth Filling

Restoration of cavities with quality materials to restore function and comfort.

🩺

Root Canal Treatment

Careful treatment to save infected teeth and relieve pain while preserving natural structure.

👑

Dental Crowns

Custom crowns to restore strength, shape and appearance of damaged or weakened teeth.

💎

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening options to help brighten your smile in a controlled setting.

😁

Braces & Orthodontic Care

Guidance and care for teeth alignment to improve both function and aesthetics.

🔩

Dental Implants

Consultation and implant solutions for replacing missing teeth with a stable foundation.
